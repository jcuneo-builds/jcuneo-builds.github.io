The problem

Resourcing ran on spreadsheets nobody trusted

Staffing decisions for 400+ people lived in disconnected sheets owned by different teams. Revenue sat in finance forecasts, people data in HR, deals in HubSpot and hours in time tracking, and none of it lined up.

Before

A spreadsheet per team No link to revenue or cost Manual month-end reconciliation Hiring before checking capacity

Leaders couldn't see revenue per head, staff cost or margin for a practice or client without days of manual work, and the numbers were out of date by the time they arrived.

After

One planning tool Synced daily from the warehouse Rev/head, staff cost and CM% live Practice, hive and client views Monthly lock and cadence

Allocation, financial and people data in one place, so leaders plan next month with the numbers in front of them and the whole company works from the same forecast.

The brief: give every practice and hive leader one tool to plan allocations against live revenue and cost, roll it out company-wide on a fixed timeline, and get to full adoption, with every direct employee allocated, not a pilot that fades.

The solution

One source of truth for people, clients and money

People Planner shows every person's monthly split across clients, their role on each one, and what that means financially. Change an allocation and revenue per head, staff cost and client margin update on the spot. Behind it, the data warehouse feeds revenue, people, deals and actual hours into the tool every morning.

Data

Five source systems, one daily sync, a closed loop

I worked hand in hand with the data warehouse team to connect every source the tool depends on, troubleshoot mismatches, and send locked allocations back into the warehouse each month.

Connecting the sources

Defined with the data warehouse team which fields the tool needed from each system and how they join: clients and deals to forecast revenue, people to levels and cost, open roles to forecast start dates, and time tracking to allocations.

Troubleshooting the joins

Chased down the issues that broke the numbers: clients missing a hive assignment in HubSpot, people showing $0 revenue per head from mapping gaps, duplicate headcount for people in two regions, and placeholder rows counted as real capacity.

Guardrails on the pipeline

Pushed for alerts when a source sheet hadn't refreshed or came through blank, tests on the calculation functions, and a time tracking import that only brings in completed months.

Closing the loop

Locked allocations now export to the warehouse each month and come back as the baseline for plan-versus-actual variance, so planning gets more accurate every cycle.

The metrics, defined once

# per person, per client
weight = allocation × role points
              Sponsor 6 · Lead 2 · Contributor 1
rev_head = Σ clients (weight ÷ group weight) × revenue
staff_cost = Σ people cost × share of client time
SCR = staff_cost ÷ revenue
CM% = (revenue − staff_cost) ÷ revenue

Role-weighted, not just time-weighted. A Sponsor carries more accountability than a Contributor at the same allocation, so revenue credit follows ownership.

Benchmarks by level. Revenue per head is compared against a target for each level, which makes it a fair read across practices with different seniority mixes.

Rules built into the tool. Non-client time is capped by level, and each client gets one Sponsor and one Lead per group, so the data stays clean by design.
