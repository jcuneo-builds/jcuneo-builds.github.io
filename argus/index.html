Situation

Lots of changes, no record of them

The team ran 100+ programmatic campaigns across hundreds of line items. Budgets, pacing, targeting and creatives changed every week, often by different people.

Before

Changes made in the platform UI No history of who changed what Manual month-over-month pulls Insights written by hand

When performance moved, nobody could quickly say why. Reporting meant exporting data, comparing months in spreadsheets and writing commentary for each campaign, which took hours every week.

After

Every change logged Scored by risk Filter by campaign, IO or line item Insights written automatically

A single change log shows exactly what changed, when, and how risky it was. Monthly insights explain what happened to performance and link it back to the optimizations behind it.

Action

How it works

Two connected parts. The first records every change. The second reads performance data and uses the change log to explain the results.

1Snapshot and compare

Python reads two SDF exports

The notebook picks the two most recent exports by date, then compares every campaign, insertion order and line item field by field.

2Label and score

Each change gets a type and a severity

Changes are grouped into 16 types, from budget segments to household income targeting, and scored Risk, Watch or Info. Anything already logged is skipped.

3Explain

Insights cite the changes behind them

The insight engine compares this month to last, finds what drove results, and pulls in the changes made that month so the commentary explains why.
