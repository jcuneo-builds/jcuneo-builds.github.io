Featured · Notion Engagement Dashboard

Is the company actually working in Notion?

The agency moved client work into Notion teamspaces, and leadership needed proof it was sticking. This dashboard tracks edit activity across every client, practice and internal teamspace week by week, and turns it into a company KPI: the share of client teamspaces actively in use.

What makes it work

  • Every teamspace classified automatically. Notion activity is matched against the CRM-fed client scorecard, so each teamspace is tagged as Client, Practice or Internal without anyone maintaining a list.
  • Client, practice and all-teams reports. One dashboard, three lenses, with week-over-week trends and the most and least active teamspaces.
  • Clean numbers by design. Self-service accounts and internal projects are filtered out server-side, matched on stable teamspace IDs so renames never break the counts.
  • Process adoption, not just logins. A recurring-tasks view tracks whether teams are completing the weekly routines the operating model depends on.
  • Fast and resilient. An Apps Script API precomputes weekly history into a cache, so the page loads instantly, and falls back to the last good snapshot if a source is unavailable.

People Planner Dashboard

Resourcing health for the whole region, in one view

Built on top of the People Planner rollout, this dashboard tells leaders whether the plan is complete and efficient: who has unallocated time, who's leading clients, and how revenue per head and staff cost compare across practices, hives and levels.

What makes it work

  • KPI cards leaders check before every lock. Fully allocated, leading at least one client, non-client time and people with no allocation, all for the month in view.
  • Practice × level matrix with a KPI toggle. Spot exactly which teams and seniority bands have gaps, then drill into the named list of people with unallocated time.
  • Efficiency heatmap. Revenue per head against level benchmarks and staff cost ratio across practices and clients, so outliers jump out.
  • Self-serve exports. Leads download a branded, practice- or hive-filtered summary ahead of their monthly resourcing check-ins.
  • Scoped at the data layer. The Apps Script backend filters by region server-side, so each audience only ever receives its own data.

How they're built

One pattern, reused across every dashboard

Join systems that don't share a key

Teamspaces are matched to CRM clients, survey contacts to companies by record ID, and people to allocations, with stable IDs preferred over names so renames never break a report.

Precompute, then serve

Heavy work runs in Apps Script on a schedule and is cached as JSON, so dashboards load in a second and historical weeks stay consistent.

Scope data where it lives

Region and audience filters run server-side. The browser only ever receives what the viewer is meant to see.

Push, don't wait to be opened

Slack alerts, reminders and exports bring the numbers to people at the moment they need to act, which is what turns a dashboard into a habit.
