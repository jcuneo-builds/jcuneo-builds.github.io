The problem

Too few answers to act on

Past survey rounds worked, but too few clients answered to give leadership a reliable read. The process leaned on manual coordination, contact lists nobody had checked in months, and results that arrived long after anyone could act on them.

Before

Stale, oversized contact lists One generic email Manual chasing Results weeks later

Surveys went to the wrong people, client partners had no view of how their accounts were responding, and leadership saw scores only after the round was over.

After

AI-curated, CP-approved contacts Live dashboard and leaderboard A/B-tested emails Automated nudges Slack alert on every response

Fewer, better contacts, two email formats tested head to head, and results visible in real time to the people who own the relationships.

The brief: sharply raise the response rate, improve contact quality rather than quantity, make client partners accountable with a visible leaderboard, and learn which email format drives more responses.

The solution

Every step of the survey, connected

I designed and ran the round as one system: Claude skills choose who to survey, client partners approve, HubSpot sends and follows up, responses flow into a live dashboard, and a Slack bot announces each one. Scores write back to HubSpot company records at close, ready for the next round.

Architecture

From inbox activity to a scored, themed response

AI picks the audience, people approve it, HubSpot runs delivery, and every response fans out to a dashboard, Slack and back into the CRM.

AI picks the audience, people approve it

The contact updater reads each client partner's recent calendar and Gmail activity and suggests missing or outdated HubSpot contacts. The recipient selector proposes one decision maker and one day-to-day contact per client and flags bounces, shared inboxes and clients with no contacts. Nothing writes to HubSpot until the client partner approves.

Delivery built entirely in HubSpot

One workflow per client partner, with a randomised 50/50 split between a personal plain-text sequence sent from their own inbox and an interactive marketing email. The landing page pre-fills the contact's email, and submitting removes them from every follow-up automatically.

A dashboard that updates as answers arrive

Scores, NPS, response rate and AI-summarised comment themes by managing partner, client partner, contact owner and client, with change versus the last round and a leaderboard ranked by response rate percentage, so small portfolios compete fairly.

A Slack bot that keeps everyone close to the client

A webhook fires the moment a response row lands, posting the score and comment to the team and leadership channel, so good news gets celebrated and low scores get a same-day follow-up.

Feedback connected to the people behind it

The dashboard joins responses to the Notion people database, fed daily by People Planner allocations. A team member view shows the client CSAT for every account each person works on, and a named-in-feedback view matches first names in comments to real people, so praise and coaching reach the right person.

AI summary of what clients are saying

Every comment is grouped into what's working and what to improve, with counts and a representative quote per theme, so leadership reads the pattern in a minute instead of reading 87 comments.

Project management

I owned the plan, the comms calendar, the HubSpot build and the reporting, and coordinated every client partner through it.

3
Lock, QA and pre-flightSeptember 18 to 25

Locked and snapshotted the audience as the response-rate denominator, QA'd duplicates and bounces, set up the A/B split, built every workflow and asked client partners to give clients a heads-up in their regular meetings.

Launched both variants, then ran the cadence: a personal push on day 3, automated nudges on days 7 and 10, and leaderboard updates shared with client partners and leadership each time.

Close and roll-upOctober 13

Closed the round, shared headline results, wrote company-level scores back to HubSpot, and captured every lesson in a runbook so the next round runs faster.
